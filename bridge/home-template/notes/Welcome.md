# Welcome to your second brain

*This is your notes folder — plain Markdown, yours to edit in any app (Obsidian works well pointed at it).*

Everything in here is read by Emrys, summarised, and put on the map. Write notes yourself, or ask him: "Emrys, write a note about…", "add that to my project notes", "remember that…".

Link notes to each other by putting a title in double square brackets, like [[Ideas]] — those links become lines on the map.

Emrys also reads, without you doing anything:

- what you have told him to remember
- your conversations with him
- the briefings he writes
- the documents in the folders listed in `~/.emrys/brain/sources.json` (Desktop and Documents to start with)

Delete this note whenever you like.
